About

I like problems that end at a register, not a guess. Most of my work sits on resource‑constrained microcontrollers, where every byte and every millisecond is accounted for — improving a device's Wi‑Fi throughput by 1200% came down to rewriting the driver layer, not adding hardware.

I've spent time on both sides of the board: writing the firmware, and then finding out why it crashed at 3am. Segger J‑Link, logic analyzers, and oscilloscopes are part of the regular toolkit — along with the discipline that automotive‑grade coding standards like MISRA C ask for.

Outside of work, I'm usually on a trail or a bike, occasionally on a badminton court, and every so often at a piano concert.
